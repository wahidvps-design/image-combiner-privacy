Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

Image Combiner & Converter (“the App”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it to combine images, convert image formats, create PDFs, or perform other supported file-conversion tasks.

1. Information We Collect

Image Combiner & Converter does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or password.

The App does not upload your images, PDFs, or other files to our servers.

We do not collect or store the contents of the files you process through the App.

2. How Your Files Are Processed

The App is designed to process your files directly on your device.

Your images and documents remain on your device while you use the App. Files are not uploaded to a remote server for conversion or storage.

Temporary files or cached copies may be created on your device while processing certain operations. These temporary files are automatically removed when they are no longer required by the App.

You remain responsible for keeping backup copies of important files.

3. Photos, Files, and Permissions

The App may use your device's built-in photo or file picker when you select images or documents for processing.

Depending on your device and operating system, you may be asked to grant permission when saving converted or generated files to your device.

The App only accesses files that you choose to provide through the relevant picker or save functionality. We do not use the App to browse your personal gallery or files without your interaction.

You can manage or revoke applicable permissions through your device settings.

4. Personal Information

The App does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personally identifiable information.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

5. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

The current version of Image Combiner & Converter does not use advertising services, analytics services, or third-party tracking technologies to track your activity within the App.

If this changes in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the relevant services and data practices.

6. Third-Party Services

The current version of the App does not intentionally send your files or personal information to third-party services for processing or storage.

The App may rely on standard operating-system functionality, such as the Android or iOS file/photo picker, to allow you to select and save files.

7. Data Security

Because your files are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers, we do not maintain a remote database containing your converted files.

You are responsible for maintaining appropriate backups of important documents and images stored on your device.

8. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or require users to create an account.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us using the email address below.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, its features, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the “Last Updated” date at the top of this Privacy Policy will be updated.

We encourage you to review this page periodically for any changes.
